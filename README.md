# 📊 Excel Minor Project – Sales Operations Analytics

### Business Data Analysis Using Microsoft Excel

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on **Sales Operations Analytics** using Microsoft Excel. It simulates a business scenario in which an analyst at a multi-category retail company analyzes sales transactions, customer information, product details, sales representatives, and monthly sales targets.

The project involves answering business questions using Excel formulas, lookup functions, data quality diagnostics, conditional aggregation, PivotTables, and advanced analytical techniques.

The workbook contains approximately 2,000 order records and supporting datasets for customers, products, sales representatives, and sales targets. It also includes data quality issues for practicing the identification of duplicates, inconsistent values, missing references, and other data-related problems.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze sales transactions and revenue performance.
- Retrieve related information across multiple worksheets.
- Identify duplicates and data quality issues.
- Analyze sales by customer, product, region, and sales representative.
- Compare actual revenue against monthly sales targets.
- Apply advanced Excel formulas to solve business problems.
- Develop practical Excel skills relevant to data analyst roles.

## 🛠️ Tools and Technologies

- **Tool:** Microsoft Excel
- **Lookup Functions:** VLOOKUP, INDEX, MATCH
- **Conditional Aggregation:** SUMIFS, COUNTIFS, AVERAGEIFS
- **Logical Functions:** IF and nested IF
- **Data Analysis:** PivotTables, sorting, and filtering
- **Advanced Techniques:** Ranking, running totals, TEXTJOIN, and multi-condition analysis

## 📂 Dataset Information

The workbook contains five main business data sheets.

| Worksheet | Description |
|---|---|
| `Orders` | Transaction details, order dates, quantities, discounts, amounts, payment status, and shipping cities |
| `Customers` | Customer details, age, city, segment, and signup date |
| `Products` | Product names, categories, costs, prices, and active status |
| `Sales_Reps` | Sales representative details, regions, hire dates, and team lead information |
| `Targets` | Monthly sales targets for sales representatives |

### Dataset Highlights

- Approximately 2,000 order records
- 300 customer records
- 60 product records
- 30 sales representatives
- Monthly sales targets from January to December 2024
- Data covering multiple Indian cities and business categories

The workbook also contains question sheets for lookup functions, data cleaning, conditional aggregation, PivotTables, and advanced Excel analysis.

## 🔍 Project Tasks and Analysis

### 1. Lookup Functions

- Used VLOOKUP to retrieve customer and product information.
- Applied INDEX-MATCH for flexible lookups.
- Performed chained lookups across multiple datasets.
- Retrieved monthly sales targets using two-way lookups.
- Used approximate-match lookups for discount slab classification.
- Retrieved sales representative and team lead details.

### 2. Data Cleaning and Quality Diagnostics

- Identified duplicate order IDs.
- Checked for leading and trailing spaces in customer names.
- Investigated inconsistent capitalization in city names.
- Identified orders referencing missing customer records.
- Checked orders involving discontinued products.
- Investigated cancelled orders with high discounts as a potential promotional abuse pattern.

### 3. Conditional Aggregation

- Calculated revenue for selected periods and cities.
- Counted completed orders with specified discount conditions.
- Analyzed average order values by product category and customer segment.
- Identified combined revenue from top-performing sales representatives.
- Compared actual sales with targets to classify performance.
- Analyzed revenue from customers within specified age groups and segments.

### 4. PivotTables

- Analyzed revenue by sales region and product category.
- Compared monthly revenue across sales representatives.
- Calculated category contributions as a percentage of regional revenue.
- Grouped order dates by quarter to analyze revenue.
- Explored profit margin by shipping city.

### 5. Advanced Excel Analysis

- Ranked sales representatives by total revenue.
- Calculated cumulative revenue over a specified period.
- Identified high-performing products within a sales region.
- Combined top-performing sales representative names using TEXTJOIN.
- Identified the highest qualifying order value using multiple conditions.
